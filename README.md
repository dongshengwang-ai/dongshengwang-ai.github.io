### Biography

I am an incoming Lead Applied Scientist at Thomson Reuters Lab and currently an AI Quantitative Researcher at Verition Fund Management's AI Lab, where I built the firm's core AI platform from 0 to 1. Previously, I was Vice President and AI Research Lead at J.P. Morgan AI Research in London, initiating and leading the flagship *DocLLM* project.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Copenhagen (part of the EU [Horizon 2020 Marie Skłodowska-Curie](https://www.quartz-itn.eu/people/esr9) program). My career spans key research and engineering roles across quantitative finance, legal-tech, and tech industry leaders including Tencent AI and the Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS).

### Core Expertise & Engineering Impact

* **Generative AI Platforms & Agents** — Built production-grade AI platforms, Hierarchical Deep-RAG agent orchestration, Model Context Protocol (MCP) integrations, and semantic feature pipelines.
* **Document AI & Foundation Models** — Principal architect of layout-aware generative models ([*DocLLM*, ACL 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.463/)) and graph-augmented models (*DocGraphLM*, SIGIR 2023) for complex enterprise documents.
* **Knowledge Representation & Evolution** — Built large-scale Knowledge Graphs at CAS (*Linked-Brain-Data*), before pivoting to Neural NLP and Agentic LLM architectures. Author of [*5 Reasons Knowledge Graph Will Never Bloom*](https://dongshengwang.medium.com/5-reasons-knowledge-graph-will-never-bloom-418601957f33) *(2021)*.
