Biography

I am an incoming Lead Applied Scientist at Thomson Reuters Lab and currently an AI Quantitative Researcher at Verition Fund Management's AI Lab, where I built the firm's core AI platform from 0 to 1. Previously, I was Vice President and AI Research Lead at J.P. Morgan AI Research in London, initiating and leading the flagship DocLLM project.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Copenhagen (part of the EU Horizon 2020 Marie Skłodowska-Curie program), where my research focused on neural natural language processing, information retrieval, and semantic representations. My career spans key research and engineering roles across quantitative finance, legal-tech, and tech industry leaders including Tencent AI and the Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS).

Core Expertise & Engineering Impact

  • Generative AI Platforms & Agents — Built production-grade AI platforms, Hierarchical Deep-RAG agent orchestration, Model Context Protocol (MCP) integrations, and semantic feature pipelines.
  • Document AI & Foundation Models — Principal architect of layout-aware generative models (DocLLM, ACL 2024) and graph-augmented models (DocGraphLM, SIGIR 2023) for complex enterprise documents.
  • Knowledge Representation & Tech Vision — Built large-scale Knowledge Graphs at CAS (Linked-Brain-Data), before pivoting to Neural NLP and Agentic LLM architectures. Author of 5 Reasons Knowledge Graph Will Never Bloom (2021).
